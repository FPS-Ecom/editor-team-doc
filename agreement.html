Editor Agreement

The standard we hold you to. Sign below to confirm you've read + agreed.

Standards

  • 5-hour max reply time during your working hours.
  • 24-hour first cut from brief.
  • 24-hour revisions from feedback.
  • 12-hour blocker flag — missing clips, unclear brief, broken link → raise it within 12 hrs of receiving the brief.
  • If you'll miss any deadline, message before it's missed with a new ETA.
  • All questions go to Jatin, not Tiffany.
  • Deliver the final export + CapCut project file in the exact Drive folder from the brief.
  • Filename must match the brief exactly (e.g. V_091_day2_FINAL_v1.mp4).
  • Color grade applied to all D-Log footage (DLog→Rec709 + Cinematic LUTs).
  • Captions burned in, manually corrected (PixSort spelling, no auto-caption typos).
  • Hook untouched. DAY X slam on Day Series. B-roll audio muted. Outfit continuity on Day Series.

Full reference: Editor Team Master Doc

Performance — 3-Strike System

What counts as a strike:
  • Missed deadline without prior heads-up
  • Silence past the 5-hour reply window
  • Repeated quality misses on non-negotiables (color grade skipped, caption typos shipped, wrong export specs)
  • Blockers raised after the fact instead of in real-time
How it works:
  • Strike 1 — flagged in writing, brief reset, no penalty
  • Strike 2 — written warning + a call to align
  • Strike 3 — we part ways and bring on another editor

The Upside

  • Part of a high-growth team — PixSort just launched, scaling fast, and we're posting 10x/day with trial reels
  • Reputation + credit — your edits live on a growing personal brand and a venture-backed app. Portfolio gold.
  • Creative freedom on top of the brief — once we know your style, we let you cook
  • Open pay conversation — perform, and we scale your rate as we scale
